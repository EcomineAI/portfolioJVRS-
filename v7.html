About Me

I'm June Vic Abello, a 2nd-year BSCS student at Gordon College with a specialization in AI systems and web development.

I design and build practical AI-integrated applications — from locally running LLM chatbots to web interfaces backed by Python. My work spans both offline-capable local AI (LM Studio + GGUF models) and cloud AI APIs including OpenAI (GPT-4o), Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude — choosing the right tool for the job. I have a strong focus on Prompt Engineering: crafting system prompts, chain-of-thought templates, and persona designs that get the best out of any model, local or cloud. I'm driven by clean, functional builds and learning through shipping real products. Currently open to freelance work, collaborations, and entry-level opportunities in AI and web development.
